Hey team, wanted to share a Global Contractor Dashboard I've been building! @channel

:tea: It's now live at https://speak-ctr-hojicha.vercel.app/ :tea:

A Vercel-hosted webpage that live-mirrors our Contractor Tracker Google Sheet, so the sheet stays the source of truth. Mostly read-only for now; you can update the Status column, but other edits still happen in the sheet.

A few things it tries to help with:
- Auto-flags contractors with upcoming end dates and updates the status to "Ending Soon"
- Sorts by urgency rather than row order, so time-sensitive ones float to the top
- Cleaner layout, easier to scan with statuses visible at a glance

Access is limited to People Ops; please sign in with your Speak Google account. If someone wants access, there's a "Request it" button on the sign-in page and I'll get a ping.

Planning to add more over time (ticketing was something Hemmi mentioned). Would love any feedback or ideas! :meow-coffee:
