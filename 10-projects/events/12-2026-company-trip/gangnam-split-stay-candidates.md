## Why split-stay

Premium single-property options (Hyatt, Parnas, Lotte, JW Marriott) are tied up in other deals or have inventory caps for Sep 14–18. Westin Josun (downtown) and Shilla Seoul (Jung-gu) are excluded — wrong area and too expensive. Splitting across two 4★+ Gangnam hotels keeps the group logistically tight on Line 2 / Teheran-ro and opens banquet flexibility (COEX, Parnas, InterContinental).

The 200+ rooms-per-property bar means a clean 115 + 115 split — no third hotel needed.

## Recommended first outreach — COEX cluster

**Lead with Shilla Stay Samsung + GLAD Gangnam COEX.** Reasoning:

- Both at Samseong station — one subway exit between them, ~5 min walk
- Walking distance to COEX, Parnas, and InterContinental, which keeps banquet venue choice open
- Shilla Stay = consistent international brand standard; GLAD = strong rate-to-quality ratio for the second block

Ask each for: Sep 14–18 (Mon–Fri, 4 nights), block of 115 rooms, tentative hold ~2 weeks.
