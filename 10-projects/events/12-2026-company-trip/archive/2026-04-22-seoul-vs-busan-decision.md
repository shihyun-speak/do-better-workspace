### Looking across logistics, cost, and experience — Seoul comes out ahead.

**Seoul gives us the most flexibility.** There are multiple hotel options across different price points, co-working spaces are concentrated in Gangnam, and it offers a straightforward arrival experience for the international team flying into ICN. Operationally, it is the path of least resistance, which matters for a week of this size.

- Gangnam has **the most hotel and co-working options that can accommodate a group this size** — several large hotels can hold all 230 rooms in a single property, and the largest co-working venues are concentrated in the same area.
    - Also central and easy to navigate for international travelers, with multiple subway lines, direct ICN access, and a high density of MICE-experienced hotels and vendors that simplifies staffing and coordination.
- While lodging costs may be slightly higher, **flights are comparable or even cheaper** due to direct routes into ICN.
    - Travel is also much more straightforward. Getting to Busan takes considerably longer and adds extra overhead. Overall, Seoul is the more predictable option, with greater flexibility if plans need to change.
- Seoul also offers more in terms of **ground transport and activities outside of the program** — extensive public transit (subway + taxi + bus) makes it easy for a group this size to move independently, and there are many areas to explore (Gangnam, Seongsu, Itaewon, Hongdae, Myeongdong, etc.) for free time, team dinners, and optional activities.
