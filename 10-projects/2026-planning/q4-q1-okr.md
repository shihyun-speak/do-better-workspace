# Q4 2026 / Q1 2027 OKR: Faster

**Goal:** Faster | POps Team Capacity | Using AI to accelerate POps work
**Metric:** Time per task on 2+ recurring POps workflows, reduced by at least 10% (before vs after)

## What's the Problem we are trying to Solve?

Recurring POps work like auditing, lifecycle tracking, and data updates can be automated, freeing up time for other high-value initiatives.

## What Success Looks like (measurable Key Results)

By end of Q1, reduce time spent on 2+ recurring POps workflows by at least 10%, with at least one manual step fully automated in each, measured by comparing before and after time per task.

## How Feasible, Desirable, Viable?

- Feasible: Medium-High, I've already shipped several AI workflows, but the technical approach for some new areas still needs exploring.
- Desirable: High, the team wants less manual work and many areas are still waiting on automation.
- Viable: High, cutting time on recurring workflows directly supports "Faster," so the team can deliver quickly and focus more on other high-value initiatives.
